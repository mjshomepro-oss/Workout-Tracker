Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

MSHI Workout Tracker is a personal health and fitness tracking application.

Data We Collect

This app collects fitness and nutrition data that you enter directly, including food logs, workout logs, and health metrics. If you connect a Google Health account, we also access activity and fitness data from that account.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to display your personal health and fitness information within the app. We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to any third parties.

Data Storage

Data is stored securely using Supabase. Google Health data is accessed in real time and is not stored beyond your session.
